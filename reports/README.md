# reports/

Generated by the pipeline. Everything here except this file is gitignored — reports are
produced from frozen artifacts on the experiment host and only redacted finals are
copied out deliberately.

`WEEK1_P1_DECISION.md` and `WEEK1_P1_DECISION.json` are rendered from a single typed
decision object, so the prose and the machine verdict cannot disagree.

If `BLOCKED.md` or any `BLOCKED_*.md` exists, it is authoritative: the campaign stopped
at a safety or integrity gate and the numbers in any other report are incomplete.

A verdict carrying `verdict_status: PROVISIONAL_MACHINE` has **not** been human-audited.
It may not be promoted to a final `KEEP`/`KILL_HARM` claim until the audit gate in
`HUMAN_AUDIT_QUEUE.csv` is discharged.
